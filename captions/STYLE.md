# Short-video house style

Taken from three reference edits (before/after showcase reels, 14–23 s each, 9:16, 24–30 fps) plus the caption-placement frame.

## Structure (15–25 s)
1. **Hook (0–2 s):** a question or bold statement as a title card over the speaker. Uppercase with one key word in a highlight box (e.g. "WHY IS LIFE'S **TASK** NEEDED?").
2. **Body:** talking head, cut tight, with no dead air. Pauses longer than about 0.3 s are removed.
3. **Cutaways every 2–4 s:** full-screen B-roll or a motion-graphic insert for 1–2 s on the key noun ("crisis", "despair", "time", "awareness"). Captions keep running on top.
4. **Payoff line:** the last sentence stays on screen fully built and fills the frame, then the video ends. No outro card.

## Captions
- **Placement:** fixed, as in the reference frame. Centred at ~65 % of the height (chest level), 1–2 lines, never over the face or in the bottom UI zone.
- **Kinetic, word by word:** each word pops in as it is spoken (scale 85→100 % over about 80 ms).
- **Size hierarchy:** connecting words are small. The keyword of each phrase is 2–3× larger (e.g. "giving / **SPACE** / to each other", "Okay / **TELL ME**").
- **House colours:** yellow #F2E500 for the main text, with a dark outline and drop shadow. The keyword can switch to an accent (white, or the brand blue) for variety.
- **Font:** a heavy sans (Poppins ExtraBold) for the main text. An optional accent font (italic serif, or a glowing mono "typewriter" style) for small connecting words.

## Motion and transitions
- **Punch-in zooms:** the speaker shot alternates between 100 % and about 115 % at each cut or emphasis, which hides jump cuts.
- **Transitions:** a quick blur/flash or glitch, a page-flip into B-roll, and a whip.
- **Motion-graphic inserts:** a dark grid floor, white circular icons connected by lines (concept diagrams), glowing mono labels and a 3D object.
- **Grade:** contrasty and slightly warm on skin, with crushed dark backgrounds and a light vignette.

## Audio
- **Voice:** clean, levelled to about -14 LUFS.
- **Music bed:** low, under the voice.
- **SFX:** a whoosh on each transition and a pop or click on the hook title.

## Branding
- **Logo:** top-left, on screen for the whole video.

# Reference set 2: style breakdown (4 before/after showcase reels)

All four are 9:16, 576×1024, 30 fps, 16–27 s long. Each shows "Before" (raw) next to "After" (edited) and ends on a 3–4 s TikTok logo outro. The breakdown below covers only the **After** edits. Frames were sampled every 0.5 s, plus frame-by-frame passes through each transition.

## R1: "Clean minimal" talking head (orange/magenta glow captions)
- **Footage:** one continuous talking-head shot on a dark set with a moody grade (deep blacks, warm skin). No cutaways, no B-roll, and no hard cuts in the edit.
- **Captions:** sentence-case, small, centred at chest level (about 60 % down).
  - **Two-tier layout:** a small white lead-in line ("This is the"), then **one huge keyword** in a bold rounded font ("cleanest", "Instagram", "presets", "these effects"), then a small trailing word below ("editing").
  - **Keyword colour:** orange→red gradient, magenta/pink, or yellow, with a soft outer glow in the same colour.
  - **Reveal:** word by word. Upcoming words appear dim or grey first, then light up as they are spoken (a karaoke-style fill). The keyword pops in with a slight scale-up.
- **Icons:** a glowing neon icon appears under the keyword when the word names a thing: the Instagram logo, a document icon for "presets", an app icon for "effects". Each one pops in with a glow halo.
- **Mood:** calm and premium. The only motion is caption reveals and icon pops.

## R2: "Hype collage" (meme/stock overlays, punchy titles)
- **Top title band:** a big heavy all-caps headline across the top third, two lines.
  - **Lines:** a small white line ("IF YOU'RE RUNNING") over a large yellow line with a black outline and drop shadow ("A BRAND").
  - **Backing:** a dark grid behind it, with a torn-paper edge separating the band from the footage below.
- **Subtitles:** small white all-caps text in a dark rounded pill ("RUNNING A", "WITH YOUR BUSINESS"), low-centre. These are separate from the big title.
- **Full-screen stock/meme cutaways (about 2–4 s each):** a man reading at a stack of books, a pointing man with a black censor bar over his eyes, and a hand fanning $100 bills.
  - **Title text:** a big two-tone title sits on top of each one ("YOU HAVE TO BE" in white, "EXTRA" in red; "ESPECIALLY WITH YOUR BUSINESS" in white/yellow with a glow).
  - **Texture:** a brown paper/cardboard texture is used as the cutaway background.
- **Transitions:**
  - **Light-leak / burn flash:** the frame tints red, then orange, then washes to bright yellow-white over about 6 frames, and the next shot fades in from the flash.
  - **Text push:** the old title slides up and out while the new word ("EXTRA") pushes in from below, with a slight motion blur.
- **Overlays on the speaker:**
  - **Phone mockups:** a phone pops in beside the mic and tilts in 3D, showing app or TikTok screens.
  - **Counter:** a big green number counts up ("1 → 1,000,000"), labelled "VIRAL GOT TAKEN" / "OUT OF CONTEXT". The number turns red on a negative beat ("OUT OF BUSINESS").
- **Pace:** fast, with a new visual idea every 1–2 s.

## R3: "Mindset mentor" (paper-cutout collage, explainer)
- **Captions:** a single word at a time, all-caps, bold condensed italic in white with a black outline. They sit at a fixed lower-centre position ("MINUTES", "YOUR", "BRAIN", "NEURON"). There are no multi-word lines.
- **Cutout stickers:** paper-cutout style stickers with a white sticker border: a brain, an alarm clock, a red target with a "GOALS" label, a fist, flame emoji, a "thinking man" with a question mark, a cyber-brain.
  - **Placement:** they pop in beside the speaker at shoulder height, timed to the matching word.
- **Full-screen explainer cards on textured paper:**
  - **Black crumpled paper:** a fist icon and a typed headline ("Do / Something") that types on letter by letter. A dotted line then draws down to a brain sticker.
  - **Blue grid paper with a torn edge:** this slides down over the top half of the frame (the speaker stays visible below). Brain stickers appear on it, connected by ">" arrows (brain > brain > cyber-brain) to show signals passing between neurons.
- **Transitions:**
  - **Orange/yellow paper wipe:** a burnt-orange to yellow paper texture sweeps across in about 10 frames, sometimes with tear lines, then cuts to black and the next shot.
  - **Hard cut to black:** about 2 frames of black before a new section.
  - **Torn-paper reveal:** a paper strip with a ripped edge slides in from the top.
- **Grade:** the edit switches between two camera angles: a front medium shot on a bright background, and a 3/4 shot in front of a dark studio with a neon "MINDSET MENTOR" sign. Some cutaway frames are desaturated to black and white with a colour sticker on top.

## R4: "Finance documentary" (Robert Kiyosaki style, money visuals)
- **Grade:** a strong green and yellow wash with a vignette. Frames are tinted lime/olive, and the speaker shot has a green halo around the edges.
- **Captions:** one word at a time in a small lowercase mono/typewriter font in white with a green glow ("money", "save", "dollars", "debt"), placed mid-frame.
- **Title card at the start:**
  - **Text:** "DOLLAR" in huge white, with "IS TRASH" in a green mono font overlaid, plus "Ft Robert Kiyosaki" in small type.
  - **Animation:** the title builds letter by letter with a typewriter glitch, and the word is swapped inside it ("trash" → "IS TRASH").
- **Full-screen B-roll and stock visuals:** close-ups of a $100 bill (a Ben Franklin texture), gold bars falling and tumbling in 3D, silver and gold coins with labels, a green line chart drawing upward, aerial mine footage, a skyline, and a vintage polaroid of a historical photo.
- **Icons:** gold line icons pop in over the speaker's chest on keywords: a document with a dollar sign for "debt", "TAX" for "pay", a hand holding a money bag for "save".
- **Transitions:**
  - **Light-leak / film burn:** an orange-yellow burn washes over the frame, used repeatedly to change scene.
  - **Paper tear:** a jagged torn edge reveals the next image (green to the dollar bill).
  - **Zoom-through:** the camera pushes into an image to reach the next one.
- **Audio:** very loud master at about -7.5 LUFS, meaning heavy music under the voice (the other three sit around -14 to -20).

## Common patterns (what the 4 share)
- **Kinetic captions:** most of them reveal one word at a time, and the keywords get extra size, colour or glow.
- **Keyword visuals:** a sticker, icon or cutaway appears on the exact word it illustrates.
- **Transitions:** light-leak/burn flashes and paper-texture wipes or tears, used in place of plain cuts.
- **Overlay layout:** either a top title band, or full-screen texture cards (paper, grid, money).
- **Pacing:** a new visual element every 1–3 s, and the speaker is never on screen for more than about 4 s without something changing.
- **Outro:** a branded handle/logo outro card for 3–4 s.
